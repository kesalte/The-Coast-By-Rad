FAQs & Guest Policies

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 NN. Early check-in or late check-out depends on availability and may carry a fee.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Complimentary breakfast is served daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM at the café hall.

How do deposits and cancellations work?

Direct bookings need a 50% deposit, with the balance paid on arrival. Deposits are non-refundable, but if you cancel at least 14 days before check-in, the deposit becomes credit for a stay within 90 days. Bookings made through travel sites follow that platform's policy.

Can I rebook my stay?

Rebooking at least 14 days before check-in is free, subject to availability. Within 14 days there is a ₱1,000 rebooking fee. Typhoon and other force majeure cases get free date changes with credit valid for 90 days.

Is there a security deposit?

A refundable ₱1,000 deposit per room is collected at check-in and returned after a routine room inspection. A valid government ID is required from every guest.

What are the pool hours?

The pool is open to registered guests from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. There is no lifeguard on duty, and children must be supervised by an adult at all times.

Can I bring children, pets or outside food?

Children 0–6 stay free on existing beds (maximum 2 per room); ages 7 and up are charged as extra adults. Pets need advance approval. Outside food and drinks in dining areas are not allowed, or are subject to a corkage fee.

Is smoking allowed?

Smoking and vaping are not allowed in rooms or enclosed guest areas. Outdoor smoking areas are provided. Quiet hours run from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.

About The Coast by RAD

The Coast by RAD is a beachfront boutique bed and breakfast and café located in San Jose, Talisay, Camarines Norte.

Established through the owner's passion for coffee, entrepreneurship, and coastal living, the business has grown from a private residence for personal gatherings into a destination offering accommodation, dining, and event services.

With its beachfront setting and welcoming atmosphere, The Coast by RAD provides guests with an opportunity to enjoy comfortable stays, café offerings, and meaningful gatherings. Its goal is to bring together the convenience of accommodation, the enjoyment of dining, and the appeal of coastal relaxation in one place.
